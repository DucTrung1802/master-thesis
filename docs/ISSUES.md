## Priority

✅ **`WID-1` was cleared the day it was opened (2026-08-14)** and it was the cheapest thing here once
priced: no scraping, one code change, and the chain loaded **3,129 series instead of 357**. Clearing
it exposed **`SHP-1`** underneath — the blocked ingest was the loud failure, the shape filter the
silent one, and 71 % of the folder had been discarded on every previous run.

⚠️ **`SHP-1`'s UNCHECKED HALF IS THE OPEN QUESTION.** `bonds`, `funds`, `economy` and `indices` carry
the same `value`-only filter and the same two-shape scraper, and none has been counted. **`FLT-1` is
the harder one**: 37 of 47 brokers' books are unreachable until a Selenium injection is diagnosed,
and no amount of re-running reaches them.

⚠️ **THE THREE OPENED 2026-08-16 (`PNL-2`, `CSP-1`, `MEM-1`) ALL BLOCK ONE THING** and were found
together when a cross-sectional layer-1 sweep was attempted on `unified_schema_all`. **Read them as
one wall rather than three items**: §2b says width is the only thing that has ever cleared a null,
and **every path to it is closed** — the grain is chosen by a name (`PNL-2`), the one correct grain
reads a single pool (`CSP-1`), and the panel that grain needs does not fit in memory (`MEM-1`).
Their fixes are cheap, medium and structural in that order:

- **`PNL-2`** was the cheapest fix in the register — derive `cross` from the ticker count the way
  `PNL-1` already made the SCORER do it (✅ done 2026-08-19).
- **`CSP-1`** is real but bounded: give `read_universe_panel` the `join()` the single-ticker path
  uses, so `--pools` means the same thing on both. ⚠️ It makes `MEM-1` worse by exactly the width
  joined — `pool__ta` at 922 channels is ~10× the design.
- **`MEM-1`** is the structural one and the only one worth doing carefully: `float32` bought a factor
  of two and is **unverified**; the real fix is to stop materialising the whole design — window per
  fold or per ticker-chunk. Until then the honest options are a narrower universe (a CLI flag away,
  ~1.3 M rows) or a machine with more RAM.

⚠️ **The cheap first step is the measurement nobody has**: `read_universe_panel` at the top ~300 with
`cs_rank_5day` fits today, needs none of the three fixed, and would put a number against §2b's `ALL`
row — which currently reads *"never ran — unverified"*.

**The four older substantive ones are upstream of the code**: `EVD-1` and `NUL-1` are about what a
null can prove; `DRF-1` and `COV-1` are defects in the *selection*, not in the stages that consume it.

⚠️ **What is left open is what is EXPENSIVE or STRUCTURAL, not what is hard to find.** The 2026-08-09
pass took every cheap thing: `EVD-1`'s one affordable null was run (it failed, `z = +1.46`), `COV-1`'s
true extent was measured and stamped into a deliverable (248 of 952 rows), and `PIP-1` turned out to
be dead code. The remainder is priced: `EVD-1`'s other 18 nulls are 1,000+ CPU-hours, `NUL-1` needs an
evaluator that does not re-run the selection, `DRF-1` needs differenced channels upstream, and
`RPR-1` is a deliberate trade-off.

⚠️ **Note what none of the fixes changed: every run in the archive still sits inside its own null.**
The `STL-1` rebuild made the VCB result WORSE, not better — `test ic −0.011 → −0.072`, `r2 −0.08 →
−0.90` — which is the expected consequence of handing an LSTM 724 channels instead of 202 on 2,918
training windows.
